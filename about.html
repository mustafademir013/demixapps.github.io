About Demix Studio

Demix Studio is a mobile application development company specializing in creating high-quality music and instrument simulation apps for iOS and Android platforms.

Our Mission

We believe that everyone should have access to musical instruments and the joy of creating music. Our mission is to bring professional-quality instrument experiences to mobile devices, making music creation accessible to everyone, anywhere, anytime.

Through innovative technology and careful attention to sound quality, we craft apps that not only simulate instruments but capture the soul and feeling of playing them.

What We Do

We develop mobile applications that transform your smartphone or tablet into a complete musical instrument. Our apps feature:

  • Realistic, studio-quality sound samples
  • Intuitive and responsive touch interfaces
  • Professional features for serious musicians
  • Fun and accessible designs for beginners
  • Regular updates and improvements

Our Apps

We currently publish 27 apps across iOS and Android, covering keyboards, strings, brass, woodwinds, regional instruments, and performance tools.

Pianos & Keyboards

Pink Piano, Best Piano, Real Piano, Simple Arranger Keyboard, Simple Org, Church Organs, Simple Harmonium, and Simple Melodica.

Strings & Regional Instruments

Simple Violin, Simple Cello, Simple Guitar, Simple Harp, Simple Baglama, Simple Banjo, and Simple Oud.

Brass & Woodwinds

Simple Trumpet, Simple Trombone, Simple Tuba, French Horn, Simple Saxophone, Simple Clarinet, Simple Oboe, and Simple Flute.

Special Experiences

Simple Handpan for immersive melodic sessions and Real Fireworks for interactive visual entertainment.

Our Commitment

We are committed to:

  • Quality: Every app is carefully crafted with attention to sound quality, performance, and user experience
  • Innovation: We continuously improve our apps and explore new technologies to enhance the musical experience
  • Support: We listen to our users and provide dedicated support to ensure the best experience
  • Accessibility: Making music creation and learning accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or skill level
